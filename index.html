How a task is scored

Each task specifies its inputs, editable deliverable, and scoring rules. During a run, the agent can call submit_score to receive feedback and revise its work. A separate final evaluation scores the files left in the workspace.

For camera reconstruction, the evaluator compares camera parameters with the reference. For animation, it samples motion over time, so a convincing pose in one frame tells only part of the story.
